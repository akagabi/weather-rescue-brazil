**Subject:** Daily Brazilian records 1885–1890, absent from EMERLAC and GHCN

---

Dear Dr Domínguez-Castro,

I have transcribed 1,194 daily station-rows from the *Revista do Observatório*,
the publication of Brazil's first meteorological network, covering December 1885
to November 1890. 835 pass quality control, giving 11,909 values: barometer, dry
and wet bulb, max and min temperature, vapour tension, humidity, wind,
cloudiness, rainfall and evaporation.

Four archives say this is new. Your PANGAEA collection has 14 Brazilian series,
the latest ending December 1856. GHCN-Daily has no Brazilian daily record before
1901. Brazil's own BDMEP begins in 1961. The oldest surviving state series start
after my period, Campinas in 1890 and Piracicaba in 1917. The one archive I
cannot check is ISPD v4, whose station list needs a login. If Brazilian stations
appear there for the 1880s, most of this email is moot, and you would know at a
glance.

A gold set I made myself proves little, so I tested the model against Oxford's
published Radcliffe series. Dry bulb 1855–1879: 274 of 276 cells. Monthly
rainfall 1851–1879: 290 of 300, counting only rows whose twelve months sum to
the printed annual total. The ten that disagree are rows where the page's own
arithmetic closes, so I read the paper correctly and Oxford has since
homogenised. Neither table was in training, and both are another country,
language, unit system and table orientation. For the Brazilian pages the check
is internal: each month prints its own summary row, and our 31 daily rows for
December 1888 reproduce it in all twelve aggregates, with barometric max and min
exact to the hundredth of a millimetre.

There is more than one station here. Sweeping 269 further pages turned up 95
monthly tables from at least fourteen sites: Cuiabá, São Paulo, Maceió, Ouro
Preto, Tatuí, S. João d'El-Rei, Cidade do Rio Grande and others, plus the
*Cruzador Almirante Barroso*, a naval vessel whose observations are marine and
presumably belong with ICOADS. I have done four of the fourteen. Most sit on the
same printed form, so the rest is mostly compute.

My question is the format. If this should reach ACRE or C3S, I assume SEF is the
target, but producing it correctly costs far less than converting later, so I
would rather be told than guess. If you want to judge the method first, name any
pages in the collection and I will send the raw output.

No institutional affiliation, and I am not seeking authorship. The data goes out
openly either way.
